## Structure recovered from the journals

The batch documents a deliberate packaging sequence: a Government Pack text master and a capability statement, then an executive Page 1, an agency alignment Page 2, and NIH-focused Pages 3 and 4. The pack is therefore more than a single speculative sentence, but these six exports do not show a completed 12-page federal package. The page-ending prompts branch to several possible next documents; they are not evidence those documents were written, submitted, or accepted.

The work organizes at least four distinct lanes:

1. **Architecture and application framing:** coherence, continuity, adaptive interaction, neurodivergent support, and EchoAuth identity concepts (J2 sections 1–5; J3 core technologies; J4 overview).
2. **Institutional packaging:** an executive summary, capability statement, agency matrix, use-case pages, company fields, and attachment plan (J2 sections 8–10; J3; J4–J7).
3. **Access research:** a SAM/UEI field and candidate NAICS codes in J3, and agency/program leads in J5. These are routes to investigate, not access granted.
4. **Health research concepts:** the NIH pages articulate potential support and research questions for neurodivergent users, caregivers, clinicians, and accessible identity flows (J6–J7). Their clinical claims need a separate protocol, evidence, and appropriate review before external use.
